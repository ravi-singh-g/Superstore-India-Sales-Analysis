# Superstore India Sales Analysis Dashboard 📊

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing the **Superstore India sales dataset** using **Tableau** to understand sales performance, profitability, customer behavior, returns, seasonal trends, and shipping efficiency.

The project transforms raw sales data into **interactive dashboards and meaningful business insights** that can help identify performance trends, profitable areas, problem areas, and opportunities for improvement.

**Organization:** Tata UISL
**Role:** Data Analytics Intern
**Duration:** 6 Weeks
**Tool:** Tableau

---

## 🎯 Project Objective

The main objective of this project was to:

* Analyze overall sales and profit performance.
* Understand customer segment contribution.
* Compare manager-wise sales and profitability.
* Measure the impact of product returns on business performance.
* Identify seasonal sales patterns and trends.
* Analyze shipping and delivery performance.
* Build interactive Tableau dashboards.
* Generate actionable business insights from the analysis.

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

* **Tableau** – Data visualization, dashboard development, calculated fields and interactive analysis
* **Microsoft Excel** – Data preparation and initial analysis
* **SQL** – Data querying and analysis
* **Python** – Basic data analysis and processing

# 📊 Dashboards Created

The project consists of **six interactive Tableau dashboards**.

## 1. Customer Segment Analysis 👥

This dashboard analyzes sales and profitability across different customer segments.

### Key Areas Analyzed

* Customer segment-wise sales
* Profit contribution
* Performance comparison between segments
* Customer contribution to overall business

### Key Insight

The dashboard helps identify which customer segments contribute most to sales and profit and which segments may require additional attention.

---

## 2. Manager-wise Sales & Profit Performance Overview 👨‍💼

This dashboard evaluates business performance across different managers.

### Key Areas Analyzed

* Manager-wise sales
* Manager-wise profit
* Profitability comparison
* Sales vs. profit performance
* Performance differences between managers

### Key Insight

The dashboard helps compare managers based on both sales generation and profitability rather than relying on sales alone.

---

## 3. Return Impact Analysis 🔄

This dashboard focuses on understanding how product returns affect overall business performance.

### Key Areas Analyzed

* Return-related performance
* Sales affected by returns
* Return rate
* Profit impact
* Comparison of returned and non-returned orders

### Key Insight

The dashboard highlights how returns can affect revenue and profitability and helps identify areas where return-related issues may need attention.

---

## 4. Sales & Profit Analysis 💰

This dashboard provides an overall view of the company's sales and profitability.

### Key Areas Analyzed

* Total sales
* Total profit
* Sales trends
* Profit trends
* Product/category performance
* Sales vs. profit relationship

### Key Insight

The dashboard helps identify high-performing and underperforming areas by comparing sales generation with actual profitability.

---

## 5. Seasonal Sales Trend Analysis 📈

This dashboard analyzes how sales change over time and identifies seasonal patterns.

### Key Areas Analyzed

* Monthly sales trends
* Quarterly performance
* Yearly trends
* Peak sales periods
* Off-peak periods
* Seasonal fluctuations

### Key Insight

The analysis helps identify periods of high and low demand, which can support better planning for inventory, marketing, and sales activities.

---

## 6. Shipping & Delivery Analysis 🚚

This dashboard focuses on shipping performance and delivery patterns.

### Key Areas Analyzed

* Shipping modes
* Order delivery patterns
* Shipping duration
* Sales by shipping mode
* Performance comparison across shipping methods

### Key Insight

The dashboard helps understand customer order fulfillment and identify shipping methods or patterns that may require improvement.

---

# 🔍 Overall Analysis

Across the six dashboards, the project covers multiple dimensions of business performance:

| Analysis Area                | Dashboard                               |
| ---------------------------- | --------------------------------------- |
| Customer behavior            | Customer Segment Analysis               |
| Manager performance          | Manager-wise Sales & Profit Performance |
| Returns                      | Return Impact Analysis                  |
| Overall business performance | Sales & Profit Analysis                 |
| Time-based trends            | Seasonal Sales Trend                    |
| Logistics                    | Shipping & Delivery Analysis            |

Together, these dashboards provide a broader view of the business by connecting **customers, managers, sales, profit, returns, seasonality, and shipping performance**.

# 📈 Business Insights

The analysis was used to identify:

* Differences in performance across customer segments.
* Managers with stronger sales and profitability.
* The relationship between sales and actual profit generation.
* The impact of product returns on business performance.
* Seasonal changes in sales demand.
* Peak and low-performing sales periods.
* Differences between shipping methods.
* Areas of strong and weak business performance.

These insights demonstrate how Tableau can be used to convert raw business data into information that supports **data-driven decision-making**.
